## Color Variants (`OreVariant`)

Interactive controls (`button`, `toggle`, `checkbox`, `radio`, `slider`, `sliderVertical`) accept an `OreVariant` enum defining rest, hover, pressed, shadow, highlight, and legible text colors:

| Variant | Usage | Rest Fill | Hover Fill | Pressed Fill | Shadow | Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `OreVariant.COLORED` | Primary CTA | `#387E24` | `#275B1A` | `#1C4A12` | `#1D4D13` | `#FFFFFF` |
| `OreVariant.GENERAL` | Secondary / neutral | `#D0D1D5` | `#B1B2B6` | `#B1B2B6` | `#59595B` | `#1E1E20` |
| `OreVariant.DESTRUCTIVE` | Delete / irreversible | `#C93636` | `#C02D2D` | `#AD1D1D` | `#AD1D1D` | `#FFFFFF` |
| `OreVariant.WARNING` | Caution / confirm | `#E1B922` | `#C09A0D` | `#C09A0D` | `#B87919` | `#1E1E20` |
| `OreVariant.REALMS` | Minecraft Realms | `#7345E5` | `#5D2CC6` | `#4A1CAC` | `#4A1CAC` | `#FFFFFF` |

> Reserve `OreVariant.COLORED` (emerald green `#387E24`) for the single primary action on a screen. Use `OreVariant.WARNING` for yellow caution actions.

## Interaction States

<ButtonStateMatrix client:load />

- **Subtly Disabled (`enabled = false, subtlyDisabled = true`)**: Keeps the variant's `disabledFace()` hue while collapsing the extrusion shadow (`rows = 0`) and lowering label/icon opacity to `50%`. Ideal for pagination arrows at boundaries or context-sensitive actions.
- **Disabled (`enabled = false, subtlyDisabled = false`)**: Switches the entire control to neutral `#B1B2B6` (`OreTone.DISABLED_FILL`), `#8C8D91` (`OreTone.DISABLED_FILL_SHADOW`), `#58585A` (`OreTone.DISABLED_CONTAINER`), and `#595A5C` (`OreTone.DISABLED_TEXT`), removing all bevel strokes and setting the cursor to `NOT_ALLOWED`.
